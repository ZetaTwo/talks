Reversing Age of Empires 2: Upgrading tools from Dark Age to Castle Age

During the past few years, I have been reverse engineering Age of Empires 2 (Definitive Edition), a game that is very dear to me.
While I do have some end goals for the project, the main driver has been to have a playground to develop tools and techniques for reverse engineering.
In this talk, I will take you through the process of how analysis of a new release of a classic video game led me to develop automation and
plugins for Binary Ninja to automatically decrypt and de-obfuscate the code including a query system for BNIL for pattern matching instruction sequences. 


Outline:
- Bio
- Background: Age of Empires 2
- Dark Age: Manual exploration
  - Finding the encryption mechanism
  - Basic Binary Ninja scripting to support
- Feudal Age: Binary Ninja scripting
  - Automated finding of parameters
  - Automated decryption
  - Leveraging Steam DepotDownloader for CI
- Castle Age: BNIL query system
  - Pattern match obfuscations with tree sitter
  - BNIL modification to deobfuscate
  - Instrumentation to verify
- Imperial Age: what's next?
  - Future ideas

Bio:
Carl Svensson, sometimes better known as ZetaTwo, is a security engineer from Sweden currently living in London. He has a keen interest in reverse engineering and low level topics. He is a veteran of the CTF community as both a player and an organizer. His free time is typically spent on long distance running or playing video games.
